# EstateIQ

**A private, encrypted inventory of everything in your household — and your firearms — for Windows.**

EstateIQ keeps a record of what you own: furniture, electronics, appliances, valuables, vehicles, documents and supplies, plus firearms, ammunition and gear. It keeps photos, serial numbers, receipts, warranties and values, tracks upkeep and renewals, and prints the inventory your insurer asks for after a loss. Everything is encrypted and stays on your PC.

> **Open beta.** EstateIQ is in open beta: almost everything works, but you may meet rough edges. Please report anything odd on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/hpmnt/EstateIQ-releases/issues), and keep automatic backups on. See [Feedback and security reports](https://github.com/hpmnt/EstateIQ-releases/wiki/Feedback-and-Security).

## What EstateIQ does

- **Household inventory**: things you own, in categories with fields of their own (appliances, electronics, furniture, valuables, tools, vehicles, documents, supplies), with photos, serial numbers, receipts, manuals, warranties, value history and who it goes to. See [Household and places](https://github.com/hpmnt/EstateIQ-releases/wiki/Household-and-Places).
- **Places and maps**: where everything is kept, as a tree (House › Primary bedroom › Fire safe), with room photos. Pin your house, cabin, storage unit or bank box on a map (OpenStreetMap), see where things are, and filter by where they're kept.
- **Ready for an insurance claim**: a walkthrough of each room to photograph and tick off what's there, how ready each room is for a claim, and a **claim inventory** PDF, room by room, with photos, serial numbers, values and replacement cost.
- **Upkeep**: schedules for the furnace filter, water heater, smoke alarms, gutters, mower, car and more, from a library of what makers' manuals and safety agencies say each needs and how often, with sources. Log what was done, by whom and what it cost. See [Upkeep](https://github.com/hpmnt/EstateIQ-releases/wiki/Upkeep).
- **Vehicles**: mileage or engine hours, service by months or miles, whichever comes first, and registration and inspection dates.
- **Documents**: passports, licenses, titles, policies and wills, what to renew soon and where each original is kept.
- **Shopping list**: supplies that are running low go on it by themselves, grouped by store.
- **The Armory**: firearms, ammunition and gear, with a range logbook, cleaning checklists from makers' manuals, parts that wear, safe checks, NFA paperwork, an acquisition and disposition record and an estate packet. It can be **hidden** behind your password. See [The Armory](https://github.com/hpmnt/EstateIQ-releases/wiki/The-Armory).
- **Coming up**: warranties ending, documents expiring, renewals, upkeep and cleanings due, warned about ahead of time.
- **Reports**: insurance inventory, claim inventory, one-item records for a police report, document lists, CSV exports, printed, saved as PDF or shared, with a log of every report that leaves EstateIQ. See [Reports and backups](https://github.com/hpmnt/EstateIQ-releases/wiki/Reports-and-Backups).
- **Android app**: your inventory on your phone, even away from home. It syncs with your PC over your home Wi-Fi, unlocks with your fingerprint, takes photos and scans barcodes. See [Phone app](https://github.com/hpmnt/EstateIQ-releases/wiki/Phone-App).
- **Automatic backups**: one encrypted file, every day, week or month, to a USB drive or second disk.
- **Updates itself**: new versions are downloaded and installed automatically.

## Private by design

- Everything is encrypted with **AES-256-GCM**, using a key protected by your master password. Nobody can read it without the password, and a forgotten password can't be recovered.
- There is **no account, no cloud and no server**. Your phone syncs straight with your PC over your home network.
- The PC only goes online to check this page for updates, and for map pictures while you have a map open. Nothing about your inventory is sent. The Android app looks up a barcode online only when you ask it to, and then sends only the barcode number.

See [Privacy and security](https://github.com/hpmnt/EstateIQ-releases/wiki/Privacy-and-Security) for the details.
